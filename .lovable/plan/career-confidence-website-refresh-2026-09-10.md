# Career-confidence website refresh

## Goal
Make students and recent graduates immediately feel that Zortex offers a credible, practical path toward their first job—without promising guaranteed placement.

## Changes
- Rework the homepage opening message around employability, real experience, portfolio proof, and interview readiness.
- Add a clear first-screen career pathway showing how Zortex closes the gap between learning and employment.
- Strengthen career-focused wording across programme, course, About, and key call-to-action sections while keeping claims honest.
- Update Zortex Externship to explain that learners build projects directly with external organizations, using **Industry-Partner Projects** instead of “Client Collaboration.”
- Give the Externship card stronger visual and content emphasis as the route from technical knowledge to workplace experience.
- Preserve all existing pages, navigation, forms, course information, and the current design language.

## Verification
- Check desktop and mobile layouts for readable, non-overlapping content.
- Verify the homepage and Externship journey in the live preview.
- Confirm all page metadata remains complete and the site builds without errors.

## Business claim boundary
Messaging will communicate strong career confidence and preparation, but will not claim or imply that employment is guaranteed.
